Welcome to Quenq

Quenq is an interactive museum of internet culture. Our library features fully functional emulators, preserved games, and rare digital artifacts, all restored to run perfectly in your browser.

Whether you're here to explore our flagship Reborn XP simulator, mess around with something in our Apps section, or dive into our Arcade of preserved Flash games, you're in the right place. No downloads, no paywalls. Just instant nostalgia.

Reborn XP Simulator

Experience the nostalgia of Windows XP in your browser! A fully functional, web-based simulator with working programs, themes, file system, and more.
