About onlinegames.stream

onlinegames.stream is a living archive of internet culture. It is dedicated to one simple idea: the web's most creative and chaotic eras deserve to be experienced, not just remembered.

We miss the days when the web was weird, creative, and full of personality. The days of Flash games, intricate fan sites, and operating systems that had soul. Most of that history is disappearing as technologies change.

Our mission is to bring it back. Not just as screenshots, but as fully interactive, living experiences right in your browser.

What We Build

We focus on three main pillars of digital nostalgia:

  • Reborn XP: Our flagship project. It isn't just a visual mockup. It is a complex recreation of the era's most popular OS with a virtual file system, working programs, and a level of detail that borders on obsession.
  • The Arcade: An ever-growing archive of the Flash era's greatest games. We use modern emulation to make these classics playable forever, directly in your browser.
  • The Apps: Home to our library of preserved software. From full console games running in the browser to classic system emulations and legendary web pranks.

The Name & The Logo

When the project was first registered years ago as Quenq, the original name was chosen simply because it looked visually perfect. It is completely symmetrical: the two qs act as bookends, the u and n are upside-down reflections of each other, and they are anchored by the e in the center.

Our logo is a slate tribute to the Umbrella Corporation. Why? Because just as they contained viruses, we want to contain and preserve the beautiful and chaotic artifacts of the early internet to keep them safe forever. But the design has a deeper layer: the structural symmetry of an umbrella perfectly mirrors a spider's orb web. It is our subtle nod to the World Wide Web, and a promise to keep it exactly how you remember it.

Kwenk: The Community

While onlinegames.stream is the arcade and the software museum, we realized that the retro web wasn't just about software. It was about the people. That is why we launched Kwenk (formerly My Quenq), our nostalgic, independent social network. It is a creative space free from algorithms and AI slop, where users can build custom HTML/CSS profiles, write blogs, and connect with friends.

Support & Involvement

To keep our site running and our archives growing, we are supported by on-site advertising. This model allows us to keep the entire experience free for everyone, without paywalls or subscriptions. It's how we fund the preservation effort.

Our direction is also shaped by our community. Many of the games in the Arcade and features in Reborn XP exist because a user suggested them in our Discord or through the contact form. If you believe in our mission and want to help us decide what to preserve next, we'd love to meet you.
