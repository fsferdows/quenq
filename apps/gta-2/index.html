About Grand Theft Auto 2 (1999)

Set in a retro-futuristic metropolis simply named Anywhere City, Grand Theft Auto 2 expanded the groundbreaking crime formula with an innovative dynamic Gang Respect System. Players worked as freelance mercenary Claude Speed, balancing jobs between rival factions—destroying Zaibatsu drug plants for the Loonies, or stealing Yakuza limousines to gain Zaibatsu favor.

Featuring flamethrowers, electro-guns, rocket launchers, armored cop vans, and tanks, GTA 2 is preserved here in full top-down glory, ready to play directly in your browser.
