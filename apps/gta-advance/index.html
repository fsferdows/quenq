About Grand Theft Auto Advance (2004)

Released in 2004 exclusively for portable consoles by Rockstar Games and Digital Eclipse, Grand Theft Auto Advance takes place in 2000, exactly one year before the events of GTA III. Playing as small-time criminal Mike, you seek retribution across the gritty streets of Liberty City following the car-bomb murder of your partner, Vinnie.

Re-creating the top-down perspective with advanced vehicle handling, weapon arsenals, and familiar faces including 8-Ball and King Courtney, GTA Advance bridged the nostalgic 2D roots with 3D lore.
