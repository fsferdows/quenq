About 2048

Created in March 2014 by 19-year-old Italian web developer Gabriele Cirulli over a single weekend, 2048 became an overnight worldwide phenomenon.

How to Play: Use your Arrow Keys or swipe on touchscreens to slide numbered tiles. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one! Reach the coveted 2048 tile to win.
