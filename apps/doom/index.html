About DOOM (1993)

Released on December 10, 1993 by id Software (John Carmack, John Romero, Adrian Carmack, Kevin Cloud, and Tom Hall), DOOM pioneered immersive 3D graphics, networked multiplayer, and modding culture, redefining gaming forever.

Fight your way through Phobos base as the lone Space Marine against legions of hellish demons.

Default Controls: Arrow Keys to move, Ctrl to fire, Spacebar to open doors, 1-7 to select weapons, Alt to strafe.
