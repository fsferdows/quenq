About Cookie Clicker

Created by French programmer Julien "Orteil" Thiennot in August 2013, Cookie Clicker popularized the entire incremental/idle gaming genre.

Click the big cookie to produce cookies, then use your fortune to purchase auto-clicking cursors, helpful grandmas, cookie farms, mines, and factories!
