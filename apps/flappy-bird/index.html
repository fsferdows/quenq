About Flappy Bird

Released in May 2013 by Vietnamese developer Dong Nguyen, Flappy Bird became one of the most viral mobile sensations in video game history before its sudden removal from app stores in February 2014.

This web edition brings back the pure, addicting, pixel-perfect experience. Tap the screen, press the Spacebar, or click your mouse to flap your wings and navigate between green pipe obstacles.

Controls: Spacebar, Up Arrow, or Mouse Click / Screen Tap.
