About Grand Theft Auto IV (Web Edition)

Step onto the grim, bustling sidewalks of Broker, Algonquin, Dukes, and Bohan in Grand Theft Auto IV. Take control of Eastern European immigrant Niko Bellic, who arrives in Liberty City to escape his past and seek the promises of wealth described by his cousin Roman.

GTA IV marked Rockstar's monumental transition to the Euphoria physics engine, realistic vehicle weight simulation, and an immersive cover-based shooting model. This responsive web edition allows fans to enjoy open-world Liberty City action directly inside modern web browsers with zero installation.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • W, A, S, D / Arrows: Move Character / Drive Vehicle
  • Mouse: Look & Aim Crosshairs
  • Left Click: Fire Weapon / Melee Attack
  • Right Click: Lock-on / Free Aim
  • F / Enter: Enter / Hijack Vehicle
  • Spacebar: Jump / Handbrake Drift
  • Q: Take Cover behind walls & cars
  • Shift: Sprint
  • R: Reload Weapon
