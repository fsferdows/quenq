About Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (3D Open World)

Experience the thrilling atmosphere of 1990s California crime culture in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas. Follow Carl "CJ" Johnson as he navigates the dangerous streets of Los Santos, battles rival gangs, evades dirty cops, and builds a sprawling empire across the entire state of San Andreas.

This web edition brings high-octane 3D open-world gameplay directly into your web browser without requiring heavy installations or downloads. Jump into supercars, customize your arsenal with shotguns, SMGs, and rocket launchers, and engage in pulse-pounding police chases through crowded boulevards and back alleys.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • W, A, S, D / Arrow Keys: Walk, Run, Steer Vehicle
  • Mouse: Look, Aim Camera
  • Left Click: Attack / Shoot Weapon
  • Right Click: Aim / Zoom Sights
  • F / Enter: Enter / Exit Vehicles
  • Spacebar: Jump / Handbrake Drift
  • Shift: Sprint
  • 1 – 9 / Mouse Wheel: Switch Weapons
  • C: Crouch
