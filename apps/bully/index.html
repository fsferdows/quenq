About Bully (Canis Canem Edit)

Originally released in 2006 by Rockstar Vancouver, Bully (also known as Canis Canem Edit) transferred the open-world freedom of GTA into the corrupt fictional New England boarding school of Bullworth Academy. Playing as rebellious 15-year-old student Jimmy Hopkins, you must survive sadistic teachers, unhinged prefects, and five cutthroat school cliques (Bullies, Nerds, Preppies, Greasers, and Jocks).

Equipped with a slingshot, stink bombs, marbles, firecrackers, and a skateboard, Jimmy battles through school pranks, gym classes, bike races, and carnival games to restore peace to the campus.
