About Grand Theft Auto V (Los Santos 3D Simulator)

Step into the modern sun-drenched playground of Grand Theft Auto V. Set in the bustling sprawl of Southern California's Los Santos and Blaine County, this 3D simulator delivers the ultimate freedom to cruise through skyscrapers, execute daring tactical robberies, and build a criminal reputation.

Featuring realistic ragdoll physics, dynamic vehicle handling, a wide selection of military-grade firearms, and an responsive third-person combat system, this browser edition runs instantly across desktop PCs, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • W, A, S, D: Move, Accelerate, Steer Vehicle
  • Mouse: Look & Aim Crosshairs
  • Left Click: Fire Weapon / Melee Attack
  • Right Click: Precise Aiming
  • F / E: Enter / Exit Vehicle
  • Spacebar: Jump / Handbrake
  • Shift: Sprint
  • R: Reload Weapon
