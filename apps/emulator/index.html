About The Console Emulator

Relive gaming history. This powerful tool, powered by EmulatorJS, brings a vast array of classic 8-bit, 16-bit, and early 3D consoles right to your browser. It supports systems like the NES, SNES, Sega Genesis, PlayStation, N64, and many more.

To get started, simply drag and drop your legally-owned game ROM file onto the application window above. For a huge library of compatible games, we recommend browsing the apps at RomsGames.

Don't forget to check out our own curated onlinegames.stream Arcade for hundreds of classic Flash games that run instantly in your browser!
