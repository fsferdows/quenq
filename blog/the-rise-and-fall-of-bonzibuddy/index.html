The Rise, Chaos, and Fall of BonziBuddy

Published: November 2026 • By onlinegames.stream Editorial Archive • 7 min read

If you used a dial-up connection between 1999 and 2004, you almost certainly encountered a purple gorilla who promised to be your best friend, sing songs, tell terrible jokes, and search the web for you: BonziBuddy.

Experience retro computing and classic desktop nostalgia!

From Green Peedy to Purple Primate

Created by Joe and Jay Bonzi under BONZI Software, the character originally started as a green parrot named Peedy using Microsoft Agent technology. In 2000, they redesigned him into a cartoon purple gorilla with a robotic synthesized voice.

Bonzi would dance on your taskbar, read aloud your emails, tell bad jokes, and download files. However, beneath the cheery exterior, BonziBuddy became one of the first widely recognized pieces of adware and spyware, relentlessly showing pop-up advertisements and tracking browsing habits.

By 2004, the FTC fined BONZI Software, and anti-virus programs flagged the lovable gorilla as malware. Yet today, BonziBuddy remains one of the internet's most affectionately remembered icons of chaotic early-web nostalgia.
