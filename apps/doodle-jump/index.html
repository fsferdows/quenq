About Doodle Jump

Released in April 2009 by Lima Sky, Doodle Jump captured the hearts of millions with its cheerful hand-drawn graphics and endless vertical platforming action.

Controls: Left and Right Arrow keys (or A and D) to move. On touch devices, tap the left or right side of the canvas.
