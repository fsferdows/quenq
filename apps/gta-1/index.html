About Grand Theft Auto (1997 Original)

Before 3D metropolises, Grand Theft Auto was born in 1997 as a gritty, birds-eye-view open-world sandbox game developed by DMA Design (now Rockstar North). Players navigated top-down cities—Liberty City, San Andreas, and Vice City—taking jobs from criminal bosses through ringing public payphones and pager beepers.

This authentic web preservation engine brings the original top-down thrill to life with responsive car steering, drifting physics, pedestrian traffic, aggressive police patrol cars, and weapon systems.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • Arrow Keys / W, A, S, D: Walk, Steer Car, Accelerate, Reverse
  • F / Enter: Carjack / Enter & Exit Nearest Vehicle
  • Spacebar: Handbrake Drift (in car) / Jump (on foot)
  • Control / Z: Fire Weapon (Pistol / Uzi)
  • H: Vehicle Horn
