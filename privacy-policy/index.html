Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

At onlinegames.stream, accessible from https://onlinegames.stream/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by onlinegames.stream and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

onlinegames.stream does not require account registration to play games, access emulators, or browse software archives. We collect minimal telemetry strictly to provide high-speed gaming and secure content delivery:

  • Log Files: Standard internet log files including IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.
  • Local Storage: Save games, high scores, and emulator states are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and IndexedDB. We do not transmit or store your personal gameplay files on our servers.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, onlinegames.stream uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Google AdSense

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to onlinegames.stream and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

onlinegames.stream's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights & GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, and data portability. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. onlinegames.stream does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at our Contact page or via our Discord community.
