About Hill Climb Racing

Hill Climb Racing is the legendary 2D physics-based mobile racing phenomenon developed and published by the Finnish indie studio Fingersoft, founded by Toni Fingerroos. First released on Android on September 22, 2012, and on iOS on November 8, 2012, the game started as a clever 5-month physics experiment that exploded into one of the most downloaded mobile game franchises in world history, surpassing 2.5 billion downloads.

Arriving alongside early smartphone titans like Angry Birds, Temple Run, and Fruit Ninja, Hill Climb Racing became a universal experience for an entire generation. Everyone had it on their phone. Whether playing on the school bus, during breaks, or at home, millions fell in love with Newton Bill, the red-hatted uphill racer determined to conquer treacherous hills from the Countryside all the way to the low-gravity surface of the Moon.

A Golden Era Mobile Classic

Hill Climb Racing represents the golden age of mobile gaming, before aggressive microtransactions and gacha systems took over. It is pure early-smartphone comfort gaming:

  • Two-Button Addictive Gameplay: Simple controls with just Gas and Brake pedals. You guide Newton Bill across wild terrain where a split-second mistake results in a hilarious neck snap or a catastrophic rollover.
  • Exaggerated Ragdoll Physics: Floating over massive hills, enduring nail-biting fuel anxiety, and balancing mid-air flips made every run thrilling and unpredictable.
  • The Classic "Just One More Run" Loop: Earning coins through air time, backflips, and distance milestones to tune your Engine, Suspension, Tires, and 4WD kept players hooked for hours.
  • Authentic 2012 Feel: The bouncy 2D cartoon art style, punchy engine sound effects, and iconic soundtrack instantly transport you back to a simpler era of gaming.

Game Controls

  • Accelerate (Gas): Press Right Arrow or D on keyboard, or touch and hold the GAS pedal on mobile.
  • Brake / Reverse: Press Left Arrow or A on keyboard, or touch and hold the BRAKE pedal on mobile.
  • In-Air Stunts & Tilt: Tap Brake to tilt the front down or tap Gas to tilt backward in the air for safe landings.
  • Fullscreen Mode: Press F11 or click the Fullscreen button below the game frame.
  • Game Settings: Click the SETTINGS button in the launcher menu to adjust audio and framerate preferences.

Pro Driving & Coin Farming Tips

  • Unlock the Moon Stage Early: The Moon stage features ultra-low gravity. Doing endless aerial backflips and neck flips rewards huge Air Time bonuses, making it the fastest legal way to farm coins.
  • Prioritize Tires & 4WD: Pure Engine horsepower will make your wheels spin out on steep dirt inclines without adequate Tire grip and 4WD traction.
  • Manage Your Fuel: Never rush blindly over blind crests. Keep a sharp eye on your fuel gauge and watch for red canisters, as running out of fuel triggers an instant Game Over.
