About Red Dead Redemption (Wild West 3D Simulator)

Saddle up and ride across the rugged canyons and dusty frontier settlements of the American West in Red Dead Redemption. Experience Rockstar's critically acclaimed western masterpiece featuring horseback exploration, six-shooter revolvers, repeating rifles, and fierce bandit hideouts.

This web simulator brings the grit of 1911 frontier life into modern browsers — duel notorious rustlers at high noon, track down wanted bounties, and protect frontier settlements with authentic western weapons.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • W, A, S, D: Move, Run, Mount Horse
  • Mouse: Look, Aim Revolver
  • Left Click: Fire Revolver / Draw Weapon
  • Right Click: Dead Eye / Sights Aim
  • Spacebar: Jump / Gallop
  • R: Reload Revolver
