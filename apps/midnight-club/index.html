About Midnight Club: Street Racing

Developed by Angel Studios (now Rockstar San Diego) and published by Rockstar Games in 2000, Midnight Club: Street Racing pioneered modern open-world illegal street racing. Unlike traditional circuit racers with invisible walls, Midnight Club granted drivers complete freedom to navigate dense urban streets, cutting through parks, subways, and alleys to reach checkpoints.

Take the wheel of exotic supercars, muscle machines, and import tuners across high-speed nocturnal freeways with nitro boosts and realistic damage physics.
