About Grand Theft Auto V (Web Edition)

Step into the modern sun-drenched playground of Grand Theft Auto V. Set in the bustling sprawl of Southern California's Los Santos and Blaine County, this browser web edition delivers the freedom to cruise through skyscrapers, execute daring tactical robberies, and build a criminal reputation without downloading large installation packages.

Our reverse engineering implementation features multiple web mirrors:

  • Mirror 1 (PlayGTA5 WASM): The authentic WebAssembly compiled engine with Scaleform loading screens, pointer lock, and live input mapping.
  • Mirror 2 (FivePlay WASM): High-availability alternative cluster server.
  • Mirror 3 & 4 (Los Santos 3D WebGL): High-FPS open-world tactical sandbox with hypercars, SWAT confrontations, and weapon progression.

🎮 Controls Guide

  • Click canvas: Lock mouse pointer (for 3D camera control)
  • W, A, S, D: Move, Accelerate, Steer Vehicle
  • Mouse: Look & Aim Crosshairs
  • Left Click: Fire Weapon / Melee Attack
  • Right Click: Precise Aiming
  • F / E: Enter / Exit Vehicle
  • Spacebar: Jump / Handbrake
  • Shift: Sprint
  • R: Reload Weapon
  • ESC: Release mouse pointer lock
