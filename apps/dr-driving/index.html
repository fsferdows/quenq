About Dr. Driving

Dr. Driving is the popular urban driving simulation game developed by SUD Inc., originally released in 2013. Unlike conventional racing games focused purely on top speed, Dr. Driving tests your precision, patience, and traffic navigation across busy city streets.

During the early smartphone boom, Dr. Driving became a global sensation passed around school buses, college dorms, and family road trips. Its memorable, upbeat jazz-funk soundtrack, rhythmic turn-signal clicks, and tight parking challenges made every near-miss and clean lane change deeply nostalgic for millions of players worldwide.

This WebAssembly port preserves the full original mobile title to run directly in your browser with no downloads, APKs, or third-party emulators required.

Key Features:

  • Built-in Unlimited Money Mod: Toggle the Unlimited Money mod in the launcher to instantly access max Gold and Coins, or reset to a clean save anytime.
  • Real Online Multiplayer: Includes full matchmaking support. Jump into Quick Matches against other players or start a Friendly Match with room codes.
  • Full Control Options: Play using WASD or Arrow keys on desktop, or steer using the authentic on-screen steering wheel and pedals on touchscreen devices.
  • All Vehicles and Game Modes: Access every car, upgrade path, and mission type including Highway, Speed, Drift, Fuel Efficiency, VIP Escort, and Parking.

Controls:

  • Steer Left / Right: A / D or Left / Right Arrow (or touch steering wheel)
  • Accelerate: W or Up Arrow (or touch Gas pedal)
  • Brake / Reverse: S or Down Arrow (or touch Brake pedal)
  • Gear Selection: Shift between Drive (D) and Reverse (R) on the in-game dashboard
